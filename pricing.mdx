---
title: "Pricing"
description: "Usage-based, to the cent. No seats. No free tier."
---

Korve bills pure usage against a public rate card — see
[korve.dev/pricing](https://korve.dev/pricing) for current rates and
worked examples. The $5/month platform minimum is credited against usage.

Budgets let you cap monthly spend per project, with alerts and an
optional hard stop.
