---
title: "Pricing"
description: "Usage-based, to the cent. No seats. No free tier."
---

Korve bills pure usage against a public rate card — see
[korve.dev/pricing](https://korve.dev/pricing) for current rates and worked
examples, and [Budgets & usage](/primitives/budgets-and-usage) for the full
meter table with exact ids and prices.

- **$5/month platform minimum**, credited against usage.
- **No seats, no tiers.** Invite your whole team; pay only for what runs.
- **Paid-only.** Operations that provision or consume resources return
  `402` until the organization's billing is active.

The rate card is also machine-readable: `GET /v1/orgs/{orgId}/billing`
returns it alongside your billing status (see
[Billing](/api-reference/billing)).

[Budgets](/primitives/budgets-and-usage) cap monthly spend per project,
with alerts at a threshold you pick and an optional hard stop.
