---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "From zero to a deployed app on your-app.korve.app."
---

## 1. Create your account

Sign up at [korve.dev/register](https://korve.dev/register). Korve is
paid-only: after verifying your email you'll add a payment method —
the platform minimum is $5/month, credited against usage.

## 2. Create a project

In the dashboard, **New Project** → pick a name, a slug (it becomes
`your-slug.korve.app`), and a runtime class.

## 3. Deploy

Point the project at a GitHub repository (`owner/repo`) and deploy. The
build runner clones your repo, builds it for your runtime class, and your
app goes live with TLS on its managed subdomain.

## 4. Configure

Set environment variables in the dashboard (values are encrypted at rest
and applied on the next deploy), watch live build logs, and set a monthly
budget cap with optional hard-stop.
